## Notes methodology
For each subject:
1. **Teaching:** Explain what the instructor actually says.
2. **Key points:** Capture the logic and examples without adding unsupported content.
3. **Scripture references:** Record citations and check passages in context.
4. **Interpretation vs. text:** Clearly separate instructor interpretation from explicit biblical statements.
5. **Source:** Link to a transcript heading and record a timestamp.
6. **Application / questions:** Practical exercises and open questions.
